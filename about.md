<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<div class="faq">

<details>
<summary>When and where does Code Club meet?</summary>
<p>Code Club meets on the second Saturday of each month, from 11am until 1pm, at Gravesend Library on Windmill Street. We're upstairs in the Community Room.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What do I need to bring?</summary>
<p>Just bring a laptop with you.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do parents or guardians need to stay?</summary>
<p>If you're 12 or under, yes &mdash; a parent or guardian needs to stay with you in the room for the whole session. If you're older, you're welcome to attend on your own, though parents and guardians are always welcome to stay too.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Where can I park nearby?</summary>
<p>The library doesn't have its own car park. See the <a href="#finding-us">map and list of nearby car parks</a> above for the closest options and walking directions.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I sign up?</summary>
<p>Use the <a href="{{ site.code_club.signup_url }}" target="_blank" rel="noopener">sign up</a> button above, or on the sign-up banner at the top of the site.</p>
</details>

</div>
