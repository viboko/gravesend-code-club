To get online during Code Club, connect using one of the three options below.

<div class="option-list" markdown="1">
<div class="option-list__item" markdown="1">
### Use your own library card 📚

Connect to **Adult Public Library Wi-Fi**. Your username is the number on the back of your library card, and your password is your PIN — the same one you use to log in to [Kent Libraries](https://kent.spydus.co.uk/cgi-bin/spydus.exe/MSGTRN/OPAC/LOGINB).
</div>

<div class="option-list__item" markdown="1">
### Borrow a Code Club card 🪪

No library card, or don't know your PIN? Connect to **Adult Public Library Wi-Fi** using one of the dummy Code Club cards below instead. If one doesn't work, just try the next.

</div>

<div class="option-list__item" markdown="1">
### Use a phone hotspot 📱

If you have a mobile device with you, turn on its Personal Hotspot and connect to that instead. This is sometimes the best option, since the library Wi-Fi can be slow.
</div>
