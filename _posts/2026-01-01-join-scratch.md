Scratch is the website we use to build our projects, so the first step is creating an account. Go to [scratch.mit.edu](https://scratch.mit.edu/) in your browser, then click **Join Scratch** in the top-right corner.

![The Scratch homepage, with the Join Scratch button highlighted top-right](/assets/img/join-scratch/join-scratch.webp){: .how-to-image}

Follow the on-screen instructions to set up your account. Your adult will need to provide their email address as part of sign-up.

<div class="callout callout--important" markdown="1">
**Important:** an adult needs to verify your account by email before you're able to share your Scratch projects — so make sure this step is done before you get too far into your project!
</div>
