## 🤔 Challenges

* *When Frankie reaches the end, can you make the game start again at the top with all the obstacles in different places?*
* *Can you add a new obstacle - maybe something Frankie needs to ride under instead of jump over?*
* *Can you make an obstacle move around? For example, could the robot walk left and right?*
* *What else can you think of?*
